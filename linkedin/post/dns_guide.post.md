If you still don’t have your own domain, this post is for you.


Sometimes we’re not afraid of real complexity. Just the version we create in our heads.

That’s exactly how I used to see buying and configuring a domain. It felt way more complicated than it actually is.

I recently got my own domain to host my projects, including Fonli — my AI-powered language learning app that will be 100% free (at least for now).

I wanted a cleaner and more professional way to organize everything, instead of relying on random URLs and IP addresses.

During this process, I realized there are a few important things developers should consider when choosing a domain registrar:
* DNS control
* domain renewal pricing
* hidden transfer fees
* security features
* overall developer experience

I didn’t know much about any of this. But I wouldn’t let it take me down. I sat down and started to study. It took me just a couple of hours to understand the fundamentals and feel confident to make a decision.

And honestly, that small step already gave me more ownership over my projects.

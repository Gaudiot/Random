I used to think DNS Records were some kind of dark magic.


Turns out, they’re actually much simpler than they sound.

When I started configuring my own domain, I kept seeing terms like:
* A Record
* CNAME
* MX
* TXT

…and honestly, I had no idea what any of them meant.

But after spending a couple of hours studying the basics, I realized DNS Records are essentially instructions that tell the internet how to handle your domain.

Things like:
* where your website lives
* where emails should go
* how subdomains work
* how services verify domain ownership

So I created this carousel to break down the main DNS Records in a simple way.

Hopefully it saves someone else a few hours of confusion 👇

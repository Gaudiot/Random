# Slide 1 — What is it?

## Title

### **Why Buy a Domain?**

## Body

A domain is your identity on the internet.

Instead of relying on:

* random URLs
* platform-generated links
* IP addresses

You get something clean and professional like:

* gaudiot.com
* fonli.gaudiot.com

It gives you:

* more ownership
* better organization
* a more professional presence

## Title

# Slide 4 — DNS Control

## Title

### **DNS Control**

## Body

DNS management becomes important very quickly — especially if you want subdomains like:

* fonli.gaudiot.com
* api.gaudiot.com

Things I checked:

* A Records
  → point domains to IP addresses

* CNAME
  → create aliases & connect services

* TTL configuration
  → controls DNS cache duration

Good DNS control gives you flexibility as your projects grow.

---

# Slide 5 — Security

## Title

### **Security Matters**

## Body

Your domain controls more than just your website.

Potentially:

* your APIs
* your emails
* your services

That’s why I checked for:

* WHOIS protection
  → hides personal information

* 2FA
  → adds another security layer

* DNSSEC
  → helps prevent DNS spoofing attacks
